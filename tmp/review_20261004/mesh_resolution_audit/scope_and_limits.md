# Tetra mesh resolution audit: scope and limits

This is an isolated pure-math geometry audit for tetrahedral coordinates and connectivity. It consumes vertex coordinates in SI metres and zero-based 4-node tetra connectivity. It does not load CAD, generate or edit meshes, call a solver, infer a crack surface, or determine physical-material validity.

For each usable tetrahedron it reports the signed volume in m³, orientation, edge lengths, per-cell maximum edge, minimum altitude, and numerical near-degeneracy. Invalid indices, non-finite coordinates, zero volume, negative orientation, near-degeneracy, and numeric range failures remain distinguishable. A nonzero volume that underflows or overflows is `UNKNOWN_NUMERIC_RANGE/UNDERFLOW` or `UNKNOWN_NUMERIC_RANGE/OVERFLOW`; its signed SI volume is null, while its edge scale, normalized signed volume over h³, and signed log10 volume magnitude in m³ remain available. Cell status counters count one terminal status per cell. The mesh comparison length `h` is explicitly the p95 of per-tetra maximum edge lengths; pooled edge quantiles are also retained so the cell statistic is not confused with the six-edge pooled statistic.

Wall thickness, local wall-normal mapping, process-zone length, and PD horizon are independent inputs. A non-empty source or definition is only a caller declaration: this pure-math module does not open or verify source bytes or prove global coverage. It therefore reports `global_wall_status=UNKNOWN` and keeps `h_to_wall` as a candidate ratio with an unknown global qualification status. A local normal projection needs a complete cell-ordered normal map and its caller declaration. Its measurement is the largest absolute projection of that tetrahedron's six edge vectors onto the mapped unit normal. Missing or incomplete normal mapping leaves the local-normal status `UNKNOWN`; the longest edge is not substituted. A local cell thickness map can support local edge/thickness diagnostics, but it does not prove global wall coverage.

The process-zone ratio is computed only from a separately supplied positive `process_zone.length_m` with a definition and source. PD `delta/h`, `h/delta`, and, when a global wall interval exists, `delta/t` are reported from `pd_horizon_m`. The horizon is never used as the process-zone length. A radial or other non-normal thickness is not transformed into local-normal evidence by this module.

`RESOLUTION_INSUFFICIENT` is emitted for a candidate scale screen only when the caller-declared scale and a named `resolution_policy` (`id`, `basis`, and the corresponding maximum ratio) are available and the reported ratio exceeds that explicit limit. Global wall qualification remains `UNKNOWN` pending independent source verification. Limits are design inputs for the declared study. A result below a limit is named `NOT_FLAGGED_BY_DESIGN_POLICY`; it does not mean physically adequate resolution. There is no default layer-count threshold and no claim that three layers, or any other fixed layer count, is universally sufficient. Non-finite or underflowed scale divisions are omitted from numeric quantiles only after recording a per-metric failure count and affected cell indices; that metric's status is `UNKNOWN`.

Surface tessellation tolerance, surface triangle-edge length, and volume-tet spacing are separate values. This module receives volume tets only and reports the p95 per-tet maximum edge as the volume spacing. Surface tolerance and triangle-edge statistics remain `NOT_PROVIDED`; a nominal 0.5 mm surface setting or a rendered surface cannot establish volume-mesh spacing or resolution.

The edge-direction second-moment tensor is a single-mesh geometric diagnostic. `single_mesh_crack_direction_bias_status` and `independent_layout_and_native_case_status` remain `UNKNOWN`: a tensor or an edge histogram alone cannot establish crack-direction fairness. That claim needs separately validated independent mesh layouts and native fracture-case outcomes, which are outside this module.

## Evidence and verification

- Read-only context: `pgfrag/schemas/pgfrag.job.schema.json`, `pgfrag/schemas/pgfrag.result.schema.json`, `pgfrag/experiments/wall_resolution/mesh_audit.py`, and the 2026-10-04 wall-resolution study and scale-design reviews under `relicweave-synlab/tmp/review_20261004/implementation/`.
- `fixture_cases.json` is hand-constructed geometry and declared synthetic scales; it is not a physical specimen measurement.
- `v1_snapshot/` preserves the exact first-version module, tests, and successful 7-pass stdout/exit/receipt. `freeze_manifest.json` also preserves the original pre-execution source/test/fixture hashes.
- V2 adds regression cases for unique terminal status counts, small and large volume range failures, unverified caller declarations, non-finite scale ratios, and the surface/volume scale distinction. Its one authorized run is preserved in `v2_run/` with full stdout, stderr, exit code, argv, JUnit, distinct case IDs, and before/after hash checks; it completed 10 passing cases and 1 failing case. The failing overflow fixture used a regular-tetra edge of `1e103 m`, whose volume is about `1.1785e308 m³` and remains below float64 maximum, so the module's `OK` result was correct. `v2_snapshot/` retains the exact v2 source, tests, diff, and freeze inputs.
- V3 changed the overflow fixture to `1e104 m` and independently asserted the analytic `log(V)` exceeds `log(float64_max)`. Its authorized run is preserved in `v3_run/` with full argv, stdout, stderr, exit code, JUnit, distinct case IDs, and before/after hashes; it completed 10 passing cases and 1 failing case. The independent overflow assertion and module overflow status/count assertions passed, then the test raised `KeyError` because it requested `unknown_numeric_range_cell_indices` at the `tetra_geometry` top level. The IDs are provided under `input_integrity` and `tetra_geometry.signed_volume_m3`. `v3_snapshot/` retains the exact v3 source and test bytes.
- V4 changes only the test's two cell-index assertions to check those documented report paths. It leaves the per-cell overflow status and unique count assertions intact, and does not change the module. The v4 diff and freeze manifest are in `v4.diff` and `v4_freeze_manifest.json`; v4 has not yet been tested.

The preserved v1 tests establish only the original implementation's narrow fixture result. V2 and V3 each contain one failed regression assertion, documented above; the corrected V4 test awaits its one authorized run. None of these results qualifies any production mesh, actual wall source, solver run, crack direction, independent-layout result, material parameter, physical sample, or RelicWeave training label.
