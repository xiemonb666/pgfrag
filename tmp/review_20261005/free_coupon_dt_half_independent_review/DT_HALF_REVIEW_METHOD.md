# Independent dt/2 result review

`dt_half_independent_review.py` is a copy of the accepted baseline adapter extended to load the baseline and dt/2 binary outputs under their separate frozen `fixed_case.toml` files. Each binary is bound by the sidecar byte count and SHA-256, each sidecar and CSV by observed bytes and SHA-256, and each native run by its launcher receipt and referenced log/source hashes. It independently reshapes the exact Julia column-major `[field, point, state]` tensor into state-major arrays and recomputes mass, full-step kinetic energy, integrated strain energy, momentum, force-density integrals, half-step boundary impulses and kinetic work, free-DOF updates, momentum identity, fixed grip path, damage, active-bond counts, and every non-step numeric column in each energy/reaction CSV.

The baseline fixed case and dt/2 fixed case must agree except for `case_id`, `purpose`, and `solver.dt_fraction_of_native_raw_bound`. The dt/2 result must contain 97 states, and its even-indexed states must share exact physical timestamps with the 49 baseline states. Pointwise displacement differences compare matching point IDs at those common times. `E_ref` is the maximum baseline CSV total energy across its states, `max_t(KE + OSB strain energy)`; both runs' energy residuals use this same fixed denominator after native CSV energies are checked against the corresponding binary sums.

The raw native stability bound is taken from the corresponding run's byte-hash-bound `summary.toml` numerical field and compared exactly between runs. The reviewer does not use summary pass/status fields and does not invoke Julia, the solver, or the native stability routine. This leaves raw-bound generation as producer-reported provenance, recorded explicitly as an uncertainty.

The all-point kinetic energy is recomputed from stored full-step `V` as the native raw solver diagnostic and separately partitioned by free-point and grip-constrained masks. The native velocity BC constrains `vhalf`; after force evaluation, stored full `V` includes `a_n*dt/2`. Fixed grip positions therefore do not imply zero stored full-step grip velocity, and physical kinetic energy at constrained grips remains **UNKNOWN**. The audit does not change the native kinetic-energy or boundary-work definitions.

The comparison figure shows only actual common-time right-grip displacement, pointwise displacement deltas, diagnostic residual magnitudes normalized by the fixed baseline `E_ref`, and the raw full-velocity kinetic-energy split by DOF mask. It does not imply convergence or physics qualification. The model is failure-disabled, NoCorrection, and uncalibrated; neither run establishes fracture behavior or material validity.

All reviewer writes are constrained to this directory. No solver, experiment, vendor, prior-review, Git, or documentation paths are modified.
