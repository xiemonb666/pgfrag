# Crack-surface architecture decision — 2026-10-05

**Recommendation to the main agent:** choose **B, a mechanics-coupled explicit crack-surface branch**, for the geometric-truth producer. Keep pinned OSB/CriticalStretch as an independently useful mechanics/event diagnostic. Do not modify that diagnostic to make a surface-fit heuristic appear native. Use A only for bounded reconstruction/export experiments. Do not switch the project wholesale to phase field now. This is an engineering recommendation; no implementation or fracture result is supplied by this review.

## Why this decision

The actual OSB storage and failure method contain positions, displacements, damage, bond lengths and activity; `calc_failure!` changes activity when stretch exceeds the threshold. There is no material-space crack location or front in that state (`pgfrag/vendor/Peridynamics.jl/src/physics/ordinary_state_based.jl`, OSBStorage and specialized calc_failure!; `physics/fracture.jl`, get_frac_params). Canonical event recording makes those transitions auditable, but cannot supply the missing geometry. A partition of points admits many separating surfaces, and partial cracks need not partition the point graph at all.

The earlier audit is directionally right, but two distinctions sharpen its conclusion:

* Explicit triangles alone are insufficient. A surface fitted every step but not used in the displacement discontinuity, interaction/support visibility and fracture evolution remains a reconstruction.
* Experimental validation is not required to define **simulated geometric GT**. A declared generative model can have exact interface identities and reference-space pairing while its material realism is unvalidated. Experimental validation is required for the stronger claim that those surfaces reproduce a class of real ceramic fractures. Neither simulated nor calibrated results become measured truth.

## Choice among the three paths

| Path | Decision and practical meaning |
|---|---|
| **A: native PD + cell-component interfaces** | Useful optional geometry plumbing test, labeled `RECONSTRUCTION_PROXY`. A shared cell-face partition can be complementary and mass-conservative by construction while its orientation and location are reconstruction choices. Cell faces must not dictate the mechanics. Never add roughness and reinterpret it as resolved physics. Do not make this a production dependency before B. |
| **B: explicit crack/front state coupled to mechanics** | **Selected.** Start with a published 3D explicit-surface formulation and a single-front benchmark. The authoritative interface is created during mechanical evolution, changes mechanical connectivity, and is retained for splitting/export. This is a new fracture backend/formulation, even if geometry ingress and event infrastructure are reused. |
| **C: phase field / adaptive cohesive / high-order seam tracking** | Phase field is a credible physical modeling family, but a diffuse damage field still needs a declared sharp-surface/partition construction for paired meshes. It does not remove the immediate annotation problem. Adaptive cohesive insertion or enriched high-order seam tracking is an acceptable realization of B if it supplies off-grid surface state and a verified implementation; fixed cohesive element faces reproduce the forbidden directional restriction. No examined source establishes that replacing OSB with one of these options is a smaller implementation here. |

A concrete starting formulation for B is Ai & Augarde's **adaptive cracking-particle method**: explicit nonplanar facets alter meshfree supports, so geometry participates in the mechanical discontinuity. Their paper treats **LEFM**, including a 3D mixed-mode example; cohesive extension and full ceramic fragmentation are not established by that paper. Use its formulation/benchmark as the first implementation target, not its title as proof of production readiness. Its meshfree support length is not the PD horizon and it is not the existing OSB constitutive model. [Ai & Augarde, accepted manuscript and primary record](https://durham-repository.worktribe.com/output/1335971/an-adaptive-cracking-particle-method-providing-explicit-and-accurate-description-of-3d-crack-surfaces), [full accepted manuscript mirror inspected](https://scispace.com/pdf/an-adaptive-cracking-particle-method-providing-explicit-and-2ki0z9yv6s.pdf), §§2–4.

If retaining nonlocal PD failure is mandatory, B instead requires a documented, energy-consistent PD-to-explicit-surface coupling. Do not invent it by applying both CriticalStretch deletion and an independent cohesive law to the same surface: that risks two incompatible damage histories and double-counted dissipation. The published XFEM–PD relocation method supports the architectural pattern, but its demonstrated examples are 2D and its crack tracking is inferred from damage; it is not a ready 3D rough-surface solution. [Giannakeas et al., 2020](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00466-020-01872-8), §§3–5. This extra constraint would enlarge the project, not be a free benefit of preserving the current solver.

## First tiny experiment: explicit 3D front, before vessel fragmentation

**Implement one bounded reproduction of the inclined penny-shaped-crack LEFM example in Ai & Augarde §4.2**, with its published geometry ratios, loading, propagation criterion and reference comparisons. Use a small coarse discretization followed by one local refinement; determine a hard memory/time/DOF cap before launch. Do not claim its budget fits the existing 5,000-point PD budget: enriched/split degrees of freedom and quadrature have different costs. Stop after a few recorded growth increments or the cap. A failed or non-growing run is an admissible outcome.

This is a declared precracked numerical benchmark, not a production knife cutter or a target fragment count. Its purpose is to show that a mechanically advancing nonplanar surface can exist independently of volume-cell directions. The implementation must retain reference-space triangles/front connectivity, both mechanical sides, creation step, and side displacement mapping; use that same surface in support visibility and quadrature. Export both reference-space sides from one authoritative surface, not two independent remeshes. Initially both sides may belong to the **same connected body**: record an internal crack, not an R2 edge between invented fragments.

Before accepting the experiment, perform one rotated point-layout run with the same geometry/load and one refinement. Record crack path, SIF/reference comparison, area and mechanical response. Compare export geometry with the in-solver surface exactly within geometric tolerance. Predeclare numerical tolerances from the selected reference benchmark and geometric kernel, not after observing results. Stop on unsupported branching/intersection rather than silently joining or deleting sheets. This proves a narrow surface-mechanics capability; it does not require a vessel, a measured microstructure, contact, full shattering or a full data pack.

Then progress to a small verified thin-wall coupon and separation-to-boundary test, carrying the same explicit surfaces into closed fragment meshes. Share the verified VesselForge material-domain/volume ingress; do not wait for an entire beaker simulation. Nucleation, branching/coalescence, process-zone law and fragment contact are distinct extensions after the single-front mechanics works. A calibrated cohesive explicit-surface model is the likely next extension where LEFM cannot resolve the ceramic process zone; selecting and reproducing that law needs its own evidence, not an unsupported claim that the cited paper already supplies it.

## Gates and permitted claims

1. **Geometry and ambiguity.** Every internal patch has an immutable surface ID, material-coordinate geometry, side signs, event lineage and error metadata. The export must be a subdivision/controlled approximation of the mechanically active surface. Changing the export tessellation must not change the fracture path. Unresolved junctions, finite damaged bands and debris stay explicit unknowns; no nearest-surface guessing of mates.
2. **Complementarity and mass.** Pairing is exact in the intact material frame before wear; current faces can separate and deform. Partition material once, using consistent cut-cell integration, and account for every volume/mass contribution exactly once, including explicit debris. Do not assign a full parent-cell mass to each split side. Check exterior provenance, closed separated-fragment shells, intersections and opposing reference normals. Zero-thickness brittle surfaces conserve solid volume; crushed/removed material requires its own ledger.
3. **Discretization and length scales.** Measure dependence on point-layout orientation, surface-front tessellation and refinement separately. Use non-symmetry rotations. Regular PD grids demonstrably have preferred crack directions, so meshfree status alone is no exemption. [Dipasquale et al., 2016](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0013794416301126). For PD, refine `h` at fixed physical `delta` first; varying `delta` changes the nonlocal model and needs a separate study. Resolve wall thickness, crack-front field, process zone and material heterogeneity independently. An initial thin-wall screen can use at least eight points through thickness, but this is a proposed starting budget rule, **not a sufficiency theorem**. The current `h=0.5 mm`, wall `2 mm` ingress probe is a geometry test, not roughness resolution evidence. Thin bodies can lack complete horizons everywhere; surface/volume integration and correction need validation rather than assuming an interior exists.
4. **Energy and mechanics.** Track external work, elastic/kinetic energy, fracture dissipation and numerical losses under the selected formulation. Count common crack area once in `Gc × area`, despite exporting two sides. Refine step/front advance as appropriate; force/energy convergence and reference benchmark agreement are gates for numerical credibility. The current OSB `Gc`-to-stretch formula is not a measured dissipation history. Native event-only pilots may continue independently while energy qualification remains open.
5. **Material roughness.** Smooth homogeneous continuum mechanics may legitimately give smooth cracks. Realistic roughness requires resolved, physically motivated grain/pore/flaw or constitutive variability, with length scales/distributions grounded in measurements or explicitly labeled assumptions. Predeclare its correlation scale in material coordinates and hold it fixed across layout rotations/refinement. Do not resample independent particle noise per mesh. Compare measured/assumed surface spectra or structure functions only over a demonstrably converged wavelength band; features below the resolved band remain unknown. Front-triangle faceting is numerical error, not material texture. LEFM omits the finite process zone; it cannot validate process-zone-scale roughness.
6. **Staged qualification.** Geometry/state consistency permits `SIMULATED_GEOMETRIC_GT` for the declared model immediately, with `MATERIAL_UNCALIBRATED`. Reference mechanical convergence permits a numerically verified model claim. Material/loading calibration and held-out fracture/mated-surface comparisons permit a stated, limited material-realism claim. Do not block the first engineering experiment on all later gates, and do not silently promote its qualification.

Phase-field fracture offers a thermodynamically organized diffuse evolution route if B proves impractical, but must still declare its length scale and sharp-geometry export uncertainty. This assessment is about the required paired output, not a claim that phase field cannot produce credible fracture. [Miehe, Welschinger & Hofacker, 2010](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/nme.2861).

## GT, R2 and RelicWeave now

* **Current native PG results:** native point/bond/event values are simulation-state truth for the executed model, subject to completed run verification. Crack meshes, physical fragment surfaces and historical direct R2 remain unknown. A first break or graph-component pair is not surface adjacency. The audited source-only fracture pilot does not become an executed result because its intact controls ran.
* **A output, if built:** exact pairing/adjacency may be asserted **inside the declared reconstruction fixture**, with a distinct `proxy_relation` namespace and reconstruction provenance. It is not mechanics-grounded R2 supervision and must not fill the existing physical/model-fracture R2 field. This allows plumbing work without pretending that the reconstruction has no internal truth at all.
* **B output after separation and partition checks:** model R2 is a positive-area original shared fracture patch between distinct final fragments, backed by the authoritative surface and lineage. Preserve this historical edge through abrasion and visibility loss. Subsequent splitting must map surface patches onto actual descendant fragments; do not make every descendant pair adjacent. Current visible compatibility is a different label. Both sides of a still-internal crack can have the same body ID and do not yet define fragment R2.
* **Consumer:** feed degraded observed-fragment geometry/features to ranking; keep clean surface IDs, pair identities, original poses, R2 and event answers exclusively on the label/provenance side. Calibrated realism and label integrity are separate gates. `synlab/truth/api.py` already requires a real new interface for R2; `synlab/observe/features.py` separates feature and answer fields. This review changes neither contract.

**Immediate allocation:** continue bounded native-event, exact-volume and void-safe-ingress work; begin one explicit-surface benchmark branch; avoid investing in A as the final fracture producer. No full-vessel batch, fixed fragment count, painted roughness or qualified full pack is implied.
