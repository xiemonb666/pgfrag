## Covariance contract

For proper orthogonal Q and translation t, push every point forward as x′=Qx+t, every vector as v′=Qv, and every second-order tensor as T′=QTQᵀ. Co-transform the crack frame H′=QH and its origin/tip; then local coordinates ξ=Hᵀ(x−o) stay fixed. Rotate the MLS reference origin the same way, while keeping reference scale, support radius, R, L, E, ν, and local KI/KII/KIII fixed. MLS values stay fixed and physical gradients become Q∇N. Volume and surface measures stay fixed; normals and traction vectors rotate.

With scalar-basis-major, node-major xyz triplets, A=I₃₁⊗Q and q′=Aq are correct. For B=diag(Q,Q), the centered physical moment constraint obeys C′=BCAᵀ and g′=Bg; K′=AKAᵀ, f′=Af, and the restored physical multiplier obeys λ′=Bλ. This assumes the moment uses r=x−c with c′=Qc+t. K is N/m, C is dimensionless, q and g are m, f and physical λ are N, and qᵀKq, qᵀf, gᵀλ are J. The engineering Voigt order in the source is [εxx, εyy, εzz, γxy, γyz, γxz], with γij=2εij; stress order is [σxx, σyy, σzz, τxy, τyz, τxz]. Convert strain and stress vectors through their respective symmetric tensors before rotating; the shear conversion factors differ.

## Findings

1. **Frame adapter required before K/f/field comparisons.** The pinned cached worker’s `_branch_values` passes world x,y directly to `branch_functions_local`, and `_reference` calls `evaluate_local_point` on the supplied coordinates. Both happen to fit the identity-frame fixture. For a transformed case, evaluate branches at crack-local coordinates relative to the transformed tip and map local gradients back with H′. Build the analytic field through the existing `CrackFrame`/`evaluate_point` contract so displacement, strain, and stress are emitted in world coordinates. Otherwise the four enriched functions and manufactured field do not co-rotate, so the proposed K′, f′, and field gates test a changed physical basis.

2. **Moment center must be explicit.** `_physical_moment_matrix` hardcodes `center=0` and uses `relative=xyz`; `_direct_physical_moments` uses `cross(xyz,u)`. Reuse is possible by passing `xyz−c` into both moment calculations and recording c separately, or by adding a center parameter. Under the translated state use c′=Qc+t. Comparing moments about the global origin has a different translation law: F′=QF and M′₀=QM₀+t×F′. About c′, M′c=QMc. The design’s “wrench covariance” gate needs to name its reference point and use the matching equation.

3. **Existing raw operators are reusable.** The current design understates available interfaces. `mixed_enriched_operator.py` exposes `semantic_coefficient_ids`, `isotropic_constitutive_matrix_pa`, `assemble_raw_stiffness`, and `assemble_raw_force` for the 27 BASE plus four GLOBAL_FRONT layout; it accepts caller-supplied semantic rows in chunks of at most 256. Its current digest is `f28f3b55…`. `efgm_elastic_operator.py` is also public and provider-injected, with a 256-point cap per call. The cached solver’s `_assemble_raw_k`, `_physical_moment_matrix`, `_direct_physical_moments`, `_force_from_traction`, `_reconstruct_field`, and `_solve_case` are private but inspectable/reusable source helpers. None supplies a complete transform-aware provider→K/C/f→KKT→field pipeline; the moving-front provider remains a scalar antiplane sampler.

4. **Keep the three orientation effects separate.** The proposed 0/+37/−23° source-cloud-only runs hold the physical state and quadrature fixed; they measure MLS source-cloud approximation sensitivity, not a coordinate-objectivity failure. The tetra study is the actual mesh/crack-front direction-bias experiment, but one step supports only a local advance claim. Preserve the same physical body/crack/loading and comparable frontwise mesh size/quality, then compare signed crack-frame advance as well as the supplied polylines. Rotating the full mesh must not rotate the physical crack or boundary state. A separate quadrature-orientation sensitivity study is absent: vary the volume/surface integration rule while holding the physical geometry and basis/cloud fixed, or use a suitable independent integration reference. Co-transforming the same quadrature with Q is part of coordinate covariance and does not measure quadrature bias.

5. **Gate values are targets, not evidence.** The query arithmetic is consistent: 1,280 rows/state, 2,560 for two states, five 256-row chunks/state; the three cloud-only cases total 3,840 rows. The 30 s, 8 MiB, and 64 MiB caps are proposals without measured K-rotation cost or an explicit output schema. Keep them labelled provisional. Net boundary force/moment can nearly cancel, so a relative error normalized by the resultant itself may be unstable; report dimensional residuals and scale force by an absolute traction resultant and moment by a stated length times that scale. The 1e−10/1e−9 tolerances likewise remain unvalidated targets; a cap, rank, support, or conditioning failure should stay UNKNOWN as proposed.
