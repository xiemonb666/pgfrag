# Mixed93 mechanics objectivity and orientation-bias design

Status: DESIGN ONLY. No provider query, field evaluation, assembly, solver, test, or render was run. The frozen toy fixture remains unqualified for material or crack-growth claims.

## Evidence and scope

The frozen case defines a 27-site 3-D source grid, 31 scalar basis functions (27 BASE plus four GLOBAL_FRONT branches), 93 vector DOFs, 512 volume rows, 768 side/cap traction rows, a 6-row physical-moment gauge, and a 99x99 KKT system. It uses E=1e6 Pa, nu=0.3, R=L=0.01 m, and pure-I KI=100 Pa*sqrt(m). The accepted cached result is a manufactured toy benchmark, not physical fracture qualification.

The fixed-BASE MLS check already compared actual provider weights and physical gradients on the 27 sources and 16 interface points. Its oblique rigid-transform residuals were 1.67e-16 for weights and 6.40e-16 for scale-normalized gradients; its mm conversion residuals were 1.11e-16 and 2.67e-16. This only establishes provider-coordinate covariance for those 16 points. It does not establish K, C, f, q, crack, or mesh objectivity.

The current moving-front provider reclassifies fixed material sources and returns scalar antiplane samples. It has no mixed93 vector basis, operator, force, physical gauge, coefficient transfer, KKT solve, tetra mesh, or advancing crack. The public elastic operator is caller-injected and capped at 256 quadrature rows; the frozen mixed93 assembly helpers are private routines. A future adapter must provide all 31 scalar value/gradient rows in the frozen semantic order, full traction integration, physical-moment C/g, field reconstruction, and a bounded KKT solve. No existing interface performs this end to end.

## Rigid-body objectivity gate

Use one proper oblique rotation Q=Rz(0.37 rad) Rx(-0.23 rad), followed by t=(0.003,-0.007,0.004) m. Transform every spatial input by x'=Qx+t: the 27 source sites, volume and boundary quadrature positions, cylinder/body geometry, crack origin/tip and local frame, front line, gauge center, and reference_origin. Rotate every normal, traction, displacement, stress tensor, strain tensor, and vector coefficient by Q; keep scalar E, nu, R, L, reference_scale=0.01 m, support_radius=0.025 m, and local KI/KII/KIII unchanged. Rotate quadrature weights by identity (they are measures); no load component is dropped or rescaled. Recompute traction as sigma' n' with sigma'=Q sigma Q^T.

With node-major vector coefficient ordering, A=I_31 kron Q and q'=Aq. Reassemble a fresh K' from the transformed provider and quadrature: compare it with A K A^T, but never substitute that prediction for K'. Let B=diag(Q,Q) for translation and (r cross u)/R gauge moments, with transformed center c'=Qc+t; require C'=B C A^T, f'=A f, g'=B g, and lambda'=B lambda. Convert engineering-shear Voigt vectors to tensors before rotation and back after. Require physical strain/stress fields to rotate, while U=0.5 q^T K q, q^T f, and the constrained energy identity remain invariant. This is coordinate covariance because source cloud and physical domain co-rotate together; it says nothing about their relative orientation bias.

Proposed gates, all on finite values: provider weights <=1e-10 and reference_scale*gradient covariance <=1e-10; raw K relative asymmetry <=1e-12; relative Frobenius errors K and C <=1e-9; relative 2-norm f <=1e-9; q and weighted field errors <=1e-8; gauge/KKT backward and energy identity errors <=1e-9. C is dimensionless, K N/m, q and g m, f and lambda N, strain dimensionless, stress Pa, and energies J. Normalize displacement comparisons by 0.01 m and gauge targets by 0.01 m. Check total force (N) and moment (N*m) wrench covariance to 1e-9 relative.

The proposed run uses one frozen pure-I load. Baseline and transformed states each evaluate the 512 volume plus 768 boundary points: 1,280 provider queries/state, 2,560 total, in five 256-row chunks/state. Assemble two raw K/C/f sets and solve two 99x99 systems. No off-grid or cohesive-point queries. Cap at 30 s, 8 MiB output, and 64 MiB explicit working arrays. Any support, rank, finiteness, or residual failure is UNKNOWN/blocked, not a relaxed gate. These are acceptance targets, not measured Krotate results.

## Independent orientation-bias experiments

A cheap MLS sensitivity precursor holds the physical cylinder, crack, loads, integration points, and boundary normals fixed, and rotates only the 27 source cloud about the z/front axis by 0, +37, and -23 degrees. The rotated cloud stays in the circular cylinder. This is three independently reassembled/solved 93-DOF cases: 3,840 provider queries in fifteen 256-row chunks, three K/C/f assemblies, and three KKT solves. It isolates source-cloud orientation sensitivity; it is not a tetrahedral or crack-growth result.

The minimum actual tetra direction-bias study requires an interface absent from current sources: a solver that accepts conforming tetrahedral meshes and fixed physical crack/loading/material state, advances one front step, and emits ordered 3-D front polylines with common-frame IDs and units. Run four one-step trajectories: an independent refined reference plus three meshes with different tetrahedral orientation (0, +37, -23 degrees about the physical front axis), remeshed to the same body/crack boundary and comparable h_min. Keep physical loads and local material parameters identical. Record solver version, mesh hashes, h_min, accepted-step state, and signed crack-frame tip advance; query/solve wall budgets cannot be truthfully fixed until that solver and its quadrature API are supplied.

Compare each output polyline to the independent reference with the pinned continuous piecewise-linear Hausdorff bounds in metres, max 32 vertices/curve and 128 total samples. Set metric enclosure atol=min(1e-9 m, 0.01*h_min) and predeclare a geometric decision tolerance of 0.1*h_min: upper<=tolerance supports the curve-distance gate, lower>tolerance detects a difference, and a straddling interval or cap result stays UNKNOWN. Also report signed tip-advance components and angle in the fixed crack frame; symmetric Hausdorff distance alone has no direction sign. For a world-frame objectivity follow-up, inverse-transform the rotated solver front and compare with atol=1e-9 m. These curve metrics cover only supplied polylines, not crack surfaces or physical bias by themselves.
