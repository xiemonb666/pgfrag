# Cached 93-DOF mixed LEFM solve

This source prepares a bounded manufactured-field solve using the frozen `a0/theta32` cache. It does not reuse the prior 405-DOF source-local enrichment or the frozen 162-DOF crack operator. Its registry has 27 MLS base scalar functions and four global branch functions, each expanded into `ux, uy, uz`: 93 coefficients total. IDs and branch-major ordering come from the pinned `mixed_enriched_operator.py` semantic contract.

The branch basis is normalized by `sqrt(r/R)`: `F1=sqrt(r/R) sin(theta/2)`, `F2=sqrt(r/R) cos(theta/2)`, `F3=F1 sin(theta)`, and `F4=F2 sin(theta)`. For `kappa=3-4 nu`, the exact branch coefficients are `AI=KI sqrt(R)/(2 mu sqrt(2 pi))`, `AII=KII sqrt(R)/(2 mu sqrt(2 pi))`, and `AIII=KIII sqrt(2R/pi)/mu`. In each branch the `(ux,uy,uz)` coefficients are F1 `(AII(kappa+1), AI(kappa+1), AIII)`, F2 `(AI(kappa-1), AII(1-kappa), 0)`, F3 `(AI,AII,0)`, and F4 `(AII,-AI,0)`; all base coefficients are zero. These follow directly from the pinned field's angular expressions and the identities `cos(theta/2)cos(theta)=cos(theta/2)-F3` and `sin(theta/2)cos(theta)=-sin(theta/2)+F4`.

The physical cylinder remains `R=L=0.01 m`, with `E=1 MPa` and `nu=0.3` as a toy benchmark. The selected cache contributes 512 volume points, 256 side points, 512 cap points across both caps, and 128 common off-grid points. MLS base values and gradients are selected with the saved cloud `unique_row_ids`; this study requests zero new MLS locations. It evaluates global branches and the exact field at those cached coordinates in chunks no larger than 256.

The stiffness is raw `sum(w B.T D B)` for full 3D isotropic small-strain elasticity in `[exx, eyy, ezz, gamma_xy, gamma_yz, gamma_xz]` order. Boundary force uses complete analytic `sigma*n` on the cylindrical side and both end caps, including every traction component. No load projection, matrix symmetrization, ridge, pseudoinverse, or mode truncation is used. The frozen operator's six coefficient rigid vectors qualify the six stiffness null modes only; they do not define the solve gauge. Instead, the six constraints are physical volume moments `V^-1 integral [u_x,u_y,u_z, ((x-center) cross u)/R] dV`, with center at the front origin and rotation normalized by `R`. Each manufactured target is the nonzero `g=C_phys*q_exact`, checked independently against the same moments integrated from the analytic volume displacement. The 99-by-99 saddle system is solved once for each of pure-I, pure-II, pure-III, and mixed `(70,40,100) Pa sqrt(m)` loading.

Before solving, raw K is diagonally scaled without symmetrization; the scaled asymmetry must be at most `1e-12`, and the eigenspectrum must have six zero and 87 positive values under `64 eps * 93 * max(abs(eigenvalues))`, with no significant negatives. The six coefficient rigid vectors must have rank six and pass the scaled stiffness-null check; the physical moment gauge must also couple to those six rigid vectors with rank six. Every case's `q_exact` displacement, all six engineering-strain components, and all six stress components are reconstructed on the cached volume, boundary, and off-grid rows and compared to the analytic reference before any case is solved. These representation checks and the independent physical-moment check use a predeclared `1e-11` relative tolerance plus a 64-epsilon SI-scaled absolute tolerance. The KKT system uses diagonal congruence scaling, and verifies the scaled saddle backward error, original physical weak residual, nonzero gauge residual, and `2U-q.f+g.lambda` identity at `1e-9`. Component-complete displacement, strain, and stress errors are also reported against a predeclared 2% engineering diagnostic limit. All six stress-component errors are retained, including near-zero normal components in the Mode III case. The result stores raw and scaled matrices, spectrum, physical and scaled loads/constraints/solutions, cached rows, exact and reconstructed fields, and full traction arrays. The script enforces a combined output limit of 12 MiB and records a conservative working-memory estimate capped at 64 MiB. Each B-matrix reconstruction processes no more than 256 rows.
